Glymphatic System: How Sleep Cleans Your Brain

Updated June 2026.

Sleep isn't merely rest. During deep sleep the brain activates the glymphatic system, a waste-clearance pathway that removes beta-amyloid and tau proteins associated with neurodegenerative disease.

Readers interested in sleep fragmentation may also read Why You Wake Up at 3 AM .

Discovery

Maiken Nedergaard's team demonstrated that cerebrospinal fluid enters periarterial spaces and exchanges with interstitial fluid through aquaporin-4 channels.

Why Sleep Activates Clearance

During slow-wave sleep the interstitial space expands, norepinephrine falls, and neurovascular oscillations increase fluid movement.

Human Evidence

Recent DTI-ALPS MRI studies demonstrate impaired glymphatic activity in insomnia and obstructive sleep apnea.

Interventions

Melatonin, sleep regularity, exercise, and treatment of sleep apnea may support glymphatic function.
